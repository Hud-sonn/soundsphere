About the app

What SoundSphere
actually is.

SoundSphere is a music player for Android. Search for a song, hit play, and it's there — no waiting for someone else's algorithm to decide it's worth recommending, no paywall standing between you and the next track.

It started as a personal project, built to work the way I actually wanted a music app to work — fast, honest, and free of the friction bigger apps add once they need to monetize. It's still that, just shared now.

You'll need a free account to get in — that's what keeps your playlists, likes, and listening history the same no matter which device you're on. Set it up once, and the app is yours from there.

Search & play, instantly

Find a track and it starts — no forced previews, no fifteen-second wait before the real song kicks in.

Synced from the start

Your account keeps liked songs, playlists, and history the same everywhere — pick up on a new phone without missing a beat.

Updates that find you

New versions download and install right from inside the app. No separate site to remember, no manual reinstalls.

See it in your hand.

Quick picks, speed dial, and everything you're listening to — right where you left it.
